About the numbers — the exact rules this app computes with

Interest: each month, every debt with a balance is charged its yearly rate ÷ 12 on the current balance, rounded half-up at the cent, before any payment is applied. Fees, rate changes and daily-interest quirks of your actual lender are not modelled — this is a planning tool, not your lender’s statement.

Payments: every debt pays its own minimum every month (never more than what’s owed). The target debt also gets your extra amount plus the minimums freed up by every debt already cleared.

The order: snowball targets the smallest starting balance first; avalanche targets the highest rate first. Ties go to the earlier row in your list.

Honesty cap: if a plan doesn’t clear inside 240 months (20 years), the app says not on these numbers instead of inventing a date. If a minimum doesn’t cover a debt’s monthly interest, the app names that debt out loud — its balance grows until the plan reaches it.

The maths is machine-checked: this exact calculation engine is verified cent-for-cent against an independently written model across 424 test scenarios before the file ships.

Your data: this file makes zero internet connections. Save writes a small JSON file to your device; Load reads it back. Browser storage is only a convenience cache.
